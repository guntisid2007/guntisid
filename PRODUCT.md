# Sid Gunti Portfolio — Product Truth

## Purpose

This is Siddharth “Sid” Gunti’s engineering portfolio: a compact, credible place for recruiters, professors, collaborators, and peers to understand what he has built, how he works, and how to contact him.

The site should make evidence easier to inspect than a conventional student résumé. The résumé remains available as a designed web page, while selected projects and research surface the underlying measurements, responsibilities, and outcomes.

## Primary audience

- Engineering recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Sid for internships and early-career roles
- Faculty, researchers, and technical collaborators reviewing his academic and applied work
- Peers and professional contacts looking for a concise overview and reliable contact information

## First-surface proof

Within the first viewport, a visitor should understand that Sid is a Purdue engineering student who works across physical systems and software, with real field and research experience rather than only classroom exercises.

## Core story

Sid makes complex systems legible. His strongest portfolio evidence combines hands-on engineering, instrumentation, data collection, and clear technical communication.

## Brand qualities

- Precise, curious, calm, evidence-led
- Young but not juvenile
- Technical without looking like a sci-fi dashboard
- Editorial without looking like a magazine template
- Distinctive without obscuring the work

## Anti-goals

- Generic dark portfolio cards
- Decorative circuit-board or blueprint motifs
- Neon HUD styling
- Inflated claims or invented metrics
- A résumé download as the only way to read Sid’s experience
- Motion or 3D that competes with the content
